Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. PassDrop is made by 5Hz development agency (5hz.io). Questions: hello@5hz.io.

The short version

PassDrop has no account and collects nothing. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • The passes you watch, your gear watches (brand, model, optional size and target price) and your settings. They are stored in the app's own database on the device and are deleted with the app, or with "Erase all my data" in Settings.
  • Scheduled reminders, held by iOS's notification system on the device.

What leaves your phone

  • A request to this website for the price catalog (a public file), a few times a day. It carries no personal data.
  • A request to each watched pass's public page (for example epicpass.com) to notice when prices or dates change. The request identifies the app by name and sends no cookies or personal data. The operators of those pages see an ordinary page visit.
  • When you tap "Buy" or "See it at …", Safari opens the operator's or retailer's site. Their privacy policies apply from there. Gear links may carry an affiliate code so the retailer knows the visit came from PassDrop; the retailer never tells us who you are or what you bought.

What we never do

  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no tracking, no third-party software development kits.
  • No account, no email address, no location.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. There is none to sell.

Your rights

Because nothing is stored outside your device, access and deletion are in your hands: Settings → Erase all my data, or delete the app. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA. Canadian residents: no personal information is collected under PIPEDA.

Children

The app is for adults buying passes and gear and is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this page and the App Store privacy label will say so before it ships.
