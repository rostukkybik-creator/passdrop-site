PassDrop support

Reminders before ski-pass price steps and alerts when the gear you want drops in price. Email hello@5hz.io and you will hear back within two working days.

How reminders work

  • Watch a pass on its page in the app. You get a reminder 7 days and 1 day before the next price step, at 9 in the morning in the resort's time zone, 3 days before sales end, and 10 minutes before an announced on-sale moment.
  • Reminders need notifications to be allowed for PassDrop in iOS Settings.
  • The app also looks at each watched pass's public page a few times a day and tells you when the prices or dates on it change.

Where the prices come from

Every tier shows the page it was read from and the date it was checked. The catalog is updated by a person, not guessed by software. If you spot a price or date that is out of date, email us the page and we will fix it the same day.

About the app's page checks

PassDrop identifies itself as PassDrop/1.0 when it reads a pass page, makes one request per watched pass per check, sends no cookies, and never signs in, fills forms or buys anything. If you run one of those sites and would prefer a different cadence or a data feed, email us.

Your data

There is no account. Settings → Erase all my data removes everything on the phone. See the privacy policy.
