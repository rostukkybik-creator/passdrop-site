Affiliate disclosure

Some gear links in PassDrop go to REI, evo and Backcountry through the AvantLink and Impact affiliate networks. If you tap one and buy something, the retailer pays 5Hz development agency a small commission. The price you pay is the same.

Commissions never change which deals the app shows, the order they appear in, or the prices reported. Every deal has to clear the same bar: at least 15% below list price in the retailer's own product feed. Pass links go straight to the operator and earn nothing.

This disclosure follows the United States Federal Trade Commission's guides on endorsements (16 CFR Part 255). In the app, deal links are labelled "Affiliate link".
